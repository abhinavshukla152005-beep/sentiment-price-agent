How to read this

Sentiment is the engagement-weighted mean polarity of the day's posts (−1 … +1). P(up) is the walk-forward model's probability that the next close is higher; E[r] is its predicted next-day return. Position is LONG above 0.55, SHORT below 0.45, FLAT otherwise — a deliberately wide band, because a 52% edge is not a trade. Support/resistance are trailing 20-day lows/highs, never recomputed after the fact. Every out-of-sample row was produced by a model that had not seen that day. P(up) is not a monotone function of sentiment: the model also weighs price state (range position, gap, volume), so a bullish day at resistance on falling volume can still score below 0.5, and the two columns disagreeing is information, not a bug.
